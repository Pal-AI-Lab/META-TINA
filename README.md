# Welcome — This is Meta TINA

In one sentence: this is an "app that makes apps," and it is itself just a folder.

The folder in front of you is a TINA (There Is No App) application: no
installer, no interface, no server. Its "program" is human-readable text,
and its "runtime" is the AI coding assistant at your fingertips (Claude
Code, Gemini CLI, or any conversational agent that can read and write
files).

**What does it do?** If you are an expert in some domain — you make
Minecraft mods, know visa paperwork inside out, or are great at teaching
baking — Meta TINA will talk with you and turn your experience into a new
TINA app, so that people with no technical background can benefit from
your expertise with an AI at their side. You don't need to know how to
code; you only need to talk about what you're good at.

**How do I start?**

1. Open this folder with your AI coding assistant;
2. Say "start".

It will explain the rest. The whole process is an interview: it asks, you
answer, it writes, you review.

**Your stuff stays yours.** Everything happens inside this folder: your
experience, your conversation records, half-finished projects — all stored
as plain text on your own computer, never uploaded anywhere. You can open
any file at any time to see what's inside — we encourage you to.

It's fine to close it midway. Next time you open it, just say "continue"
and it will tell you where you left off.
