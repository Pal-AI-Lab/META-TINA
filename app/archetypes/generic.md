# Archetype: Generic (Fallback)

## Purpose

The fallback skeleton for when none of the four archetypes fits.

First ask yourself: does it not fit because the idea is genuinely novel,
or because "what is the artifact?" wasn't thought through at admission?
If it's the latter, going back to the admission phase for another
conversation saves more time than forcing the fallback.

## Minimal skeleton

app/{guidance documents}; state/{JOURNAL.md}; plus all of the spec's
MUSTs and the full set of conventions from workspace-conventions.md
(partitioning, journal, startup, first run, stuck protocol).

## Extra homework

With no archetype-prefabricated loop and DoD, you must define two things
from scratch with the expert during extraction, and write them into its
AGENTS.md:

- **P (process)**: from opening to completion, what does one typical use
  look like?
- **V (verification)**: what counts as done? Who judges, and on what
  basis?

If these two can't be pinned down, don't continue — it usually means the
admission criteria never really passed.

## Record-keeping

Every time a project starts as generic, note its shape in
state/insights.md. A generic shape that keeps recurring is the next
archetype that should be added — this is how the archetype library grows.
