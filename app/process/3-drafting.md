# Phase 3 — Drafting & Review

## Purpose

Turn knowledge.md into finished guidance documents for the output TINA,
reviewed by the expert until they approve.

## Steps

1. **Plan the document list.** Following the skeleton notes in the
   archetype file, list which guidance documents the output TINA's app
   area needs and what each covers; walk the expert through this list
   before writing.
2. **Draft one by one.** Write according to app/craft/voice.md (remember:
   the people the output TINA serves know even less than the expert in
   front of you); tag every domain judgment's source per M2; anything you
   add from your own knowledge gets tagged
   [PENDING EXPERT CONFIRMATION] without exception.
3. **Review loop.** Ask the expert to read. Many experts dislike reading
   long documents — you may read passages aloud and confirm verbally, but
   every confirmation must land in the text. Eliminate
   [PENDING EXPERT CONFIRMATION] tags one by one.
4. **More than one round.** The review loop continues until the expert
   says something equivalent to "this is what I would teach."

## Common pitfalls

- Piling on jargon to sound professional. The output TINA's readers are
  laypeople; clarity IS professionalism.
- Quietly writing in "wisdom" the expert never said. That is the M2 red
  line.
- Writing guidance documents like textbooks. They are field manuals for
  an agent: decision points, criteria, pitfalls and escapes take priority
  over background knowledge.

## Outputs

- All finished drafts under state/projects/<project-name>/drafts/;
- update PROJECT.md; commit.
