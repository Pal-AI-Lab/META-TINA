# Workflow — Six-Phase Overview

Turning an expert's experience into a deliverable TINA takes six steps:

1. **Admission interview** (1-admission.md) — Is this idea a good fit for
   a TINA? Pick the archetype, open the project — or gracefully turn it
   away.
2. **Knowledge extraction** (2-extraction.md) — Draw the expert's
   experience out and organize it into structured notes.
3. **Drafting & review** (3-drafting.md) — You draft the guidance
   documents, the expert marks them up, until they say "this is what I
   would teach."
4. **Assembly** (4-assembly.md) — Assemble the approved drafts into a
   complete, conformant workspace.
5. **Test drive** (5-testdrive.md) — In a clean context, play the role of
   an end user and actually run it.
6. **Release** (6-release.md) — Clean up, deliver, and feed the
   retrospective back.

## Rules

- **Read before you act.** Before entering any phase, read its document
  in full.
- **Persist on every shift.** On each phase transition, update the
  project's PROJECT.md and commit (M6).
- **Going back is allowed.** The expert may ask to return to a previous
  phase at any time; record the rollback in PROJECT.md too.
- **Only two hard gates.** M1 (no assembly without passing admission) and
  M4 (no release without passing the test drive) are non-negotiable; the
  order of other phases may flex at the expert's request, but record the
  reason.
- **Confirm at each phase's close.** In two or three sentences, summarize
  for the expert what this phase accomplished and what comes next; get
  their approval before shifting.
